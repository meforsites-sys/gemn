Unit 1 — Health and Accidents

الدرس الأول والمفردات (hurt / sore / pain)

hurt (فعل): يتبع زمن الجملة وموقعه بعد الفاعل (My feet hurt today). sore (صفة): تأتي غالباً بعد أفعال الكينونة (My feet are sore). pain (اسم): يأتي بعد أدوات التعريف/التنكير أو صفات التملك (I have a pain in my stomach).

تمرين للطالب:

1. My feet __________ today.
2. My feet are __________.
3. I have a __________ in my stomach.
تذكر: hurt فعل، sore صفة، pain اسم بعد a.
1. hurt — 2. sore — 3. pain

الأفعال العبارية، الماضي البسيط والمستمر، البادئات، والإنشاء

  • Phrasal Verbs: إذا كان المفعول به ضميراً (it, them, him) يوضع بين الفعل وحرف الجر (give it up).
  • Past Simple & Continuous: While / As + ماضي مستمر (was/were + V-ing)؛ و When / And + ماضي بسيط.
  • Prefixes: ir- مع R، im- مع M/P (شواذ: unpopular, unpleasant)، il- مع L، in- للمجموعة المحددة، un- للمجموعة العامة.
How to reduce car accidents: There are thousands of car accidents a year which cause serious injuries. Drivers must not get distracted while driving and should focus safely on the road. It is important to prevent using phones for texting or emails. Those who break the law should be punished and may have their licence permanently banned. Consequently, following map apps carefully helps reduce the risk of accidents.
Unit 2 — Law and Order

الإلزام والمنع، النصيحة والطلبات والاقتراحات والعروض، التوقع، والأفعال السببية

  • must / have to / need to: إلزام — mustn't: منع قاطع — don't have to / needn't: عدم ضرورة.
  • Expectation: نحذف I think ... will ونعوّض بـ should، ونحذف I don't think ... will ونعوّض بـ shouldn't (و is تصبح be).
  • Causative Verbs: have / get + مفعول به + P.P.؛ و make + مفعول به + فعل مجرد.

تمرين التوقع (37/D — ليحله الطالب):

You have always wanted to fly. I think you will like the Air Force.
You should like the Air Force.
Unit 3 — Careers, Conditionals, Reported Speech & Regret

الجمل الشرطية الأربع، الكلام المنقول، والندم

  • Zero: If + مضارع بسيط ⬅ مضارع بسيط. First: If + مضارع بسيط ⬅ will/can/may + مجرد.
  • Second: If + ماضي بسيط ⬅ would/could/might + مجرد. Third: If + ماضي تام ⬅ would/could have + P.P.
  • Reported Speech & Regret: إرجاع الزمن خطوة للماضي في المنقول؛ و I wish / If only + ماضي تام للندم.

تمرين الندم (58/A — ليحله الطالب):

He didn't study hard and now he has failed the exam.
If only he had studied harder.
Unit 4 — Revision & Review

مفردات المشاريع الناشئة وتقنيات اللياقة البدنية والإنشاء

start-up business, business plan, investor, loan, profit, marketing, target market, fitness tech, wearable device, app, track, goal, motivation, feature.

Starting a Start-up Business: Starting a start-up business can be challenging, but careful planning makes success much more likely. First, entrepreneurs need a clear idea that solves a real problem or meets a growing demand in the market. Writing a good business plan helps them organize their goals, estimate costs, and attract investors or get a loan. Effective marketing is also essential so that customers know about the new product or service. Although there are risks and hard work at the beginning, a well-managed start-up can grow steadily and become profitable over time.
